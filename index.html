About the app

The Scheme-Cum-Plan Builder helps Zimbabwean teachers produce integrated planning documents that combine schemes of work with detailed lesson plans — the scheme-cum-planning approach from the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education Teacher Training Script.

It enforces vertical alignment from term objectives down to daily activities: SMART objectives across knowledge, skills, values and indicators; methodologies matched to objectives (project-based, inquiry, collaborative, experiential, storytelling, community-based); indigenous knowledge and school-based projects woven throughout — not added as afterthoughts; and assessment covering observation, method, criteria and post-lesson reflection.

Policy basis: Education Act Chapter 25:04 Section 62 • Director’s Circular No. 14 of 2019 • Secretary’s Circular No. 3 of 2021.

Privacy: there are no accounts on a server. Teacher profiles live only in this browser (PIN-protected on shared computers), and all schemes and uploaded documents stay on your device. Use JSON backup to move work between devices.
